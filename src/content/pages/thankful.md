We imagined this page with Supernova's former art and tech director and co-founder, Andrey K., back in the site's first iterations in 2020 — but never got around to implementing it, always finding other tasks more urgent. It's important not to forget to say thank you in time, and now we won't waste another moment. Here you'll find our tributes and gratitude to our partners, friends, allies, and teachers. Consider this our gratitude journal — one that happens to be public.

And the first thank you goes to Andrey himself. Bro, none of this would have happened without you. SN will always have your design gene, and we are thankful for that.
